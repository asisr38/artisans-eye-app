# Kathmandu Valley room references

Reference pass: September 7, 2026. User direction: contemporary Bhaktapur gallery, informed by art museums and architecture in Bhaktapur, Kathmandu and Lalitpur.

| Reference                                                                                               | Observed architectural character                                                                           | Original adaptation in the room                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [KVPT Architectural Galleries, Patan Museum](https://www.kvptnepal.org/project/architectural-galleries) | Museum spaces across Mulchowk and Sundari Chowk; architectural woodwork and material fragments as exhibits | Repeated timber bays, bracketed columns, a quiet central route and space for close viewing                       |
| [National Art Museum, Bhaktapur](https://www.nationalartmuseum.gov.np/)                                 | Woodcraft collection, peacock-window tradition and layered carved door surrounds                           | Deeper window reveals, diamond screens, fan-shaped heads and layered jambs with restrained geometric details     |
| [Pashupati Museum restoration, Architecture Asia](https://www.architecture-asia.com/Data/View/692)      | Lime plaster, telia terracotta, closely spaced timber joists, latticework and warm museum lighting         | Pale plaster above brick dado, terracotta side paving, closely spaced roof joists and small gallery light tracks |

The result interprets material, rhythm and craft detail. It does not reproduce a specific building, sacred carving or historical layout. Reference photographs were inspected for inspiration and are not bundled as project assets.

The same Blender architecture and shared lighting serve the homepage gallery and `/room`. A framed skylight and central stone path support the contemporary gallery direction. Product photography remains independent from the scenery.
