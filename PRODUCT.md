# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People discovering Nepali handcraft, including collectors, practitioners, and buyers furnishing a considered space. They need to inspect a specific piece and understand its maker before making an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

A curated Nepali handcraft gallery. Continue the existing single-artifact inquiry hypothesis with a complete contemporary Bhaktapur gallery experience. Revamp authorized September 7, 2026; contemporary Bhaktapur gallery direction explicitly chosen by the user.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, tactile, attentive. The physical references are warm plaster, dark timber joinery, and a carefully lit painting.

## Anti-references

Generic marketplace grids, simulated NFT commerce, unskippable spectacle, and decorative effects that obscure the piece. Do not invent artisan identities or product provenance.

## Design Principles

- The work is immediately visible.
- Spatial exploration and close inspection serve different needs.
- Keep one direct inquiry path.
- Architecture is original exhibition scenery, not a claimed reconstruction of a heritage building.
- Preserve the original artifact photographs and existing work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard chapter navigation, an image alternative to WebGL, reduced-motion behavior, mobile touch support, visible focus, and clear form errors. Accessibility behavior already established in the repository remains a requirement.
